Who We Are

MORE THAN JUST A GAME

Fire Arm is a Roblox game community built around an interactive gaming experience. Our goal is to create an environment where players can enjoy the game, interact with others and take part in a well-organized community.

A good community needs more than a game. It needs people who communicate, help others, understand the rules and take responsibility for their actions. That is where our staff team comes in.

We want staff members who are willing to learn, remain professional and help make the experience better for everyone.

  • A community built around fair treatment
  • Staff who communicate respectfully
  • Clear expectations and responsibilities
  • Opportunities to learn and improve
  • A focus on a welcoming player experience

Before Applying

STAFF REQUIREMENTS

Applying for staff is a responsibility. Before submitting your application, make sure you understand what the team expects from its members.

What We Look For

  • Respectful communication
  • Honesty and reliability
  • Good judgement
  • Ability to follow instructions
  • Willingness to learn
  • Fair treatment of players
  • Responsible use of staff permissions
  • Ability to work with a team

What You Should Understand

Staff members are expected to follow the same rules they enforce. Having a staff role does not mean someone is above the community rules.

Decisions should be based on available information, not personal arguments, friendships or assumptions.

Staff information should be handled responsibly. Private reports and internal discussions should not be shared casually.

Applicants should answer the questionnaire honestly and provide their own responses.

Need Help?

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Here are some answers to common questions about joining the Fire Arm staff team.

How do I apply for staff?

Click any Sign Up button on this website. It will open the Fire Arm staff application questionnaire. Complete the required fields and submit your answers.

Does submitting an application guarantee acceptance?

No. Submitting an application means that your answers can be considered. Acceptance depends on the review process and the team's current needs.

What should I include in my answers?

Answer each question honestly and clearly. Use your own words and explain your reasoning when a question asks you to describe a situation.

Can I apply if I have no previous staff experience?

You can submit an application if you are interested in learning. Be honest about your experience and explain what you can contribute to the team.

How long does the review take?

Review times can vary depending on the number of applications and staff availability. Follow any official updates provided by the team.

Can I change my application after submitting it?

This depends on how the application system is managed. If you need to correct something, contact the appropriate staff member through an official community channel.

What happens if my application is declined?

A declined application does not mean you cannot improve. Review any feedback provided and follow the team's guidance about future applications.

What makes a good staff member?

Good staff members communicate clearly, follow the rules, treat players fairly, remain calm and use their permissions responsibly.

Do staff members have to follow the rules?

Yes. Staff members should follow the community rules and set a positive example through their conduct.

Where does the Sign Up button take me?

It opens application.html, which should be the existing questionnaire file in the same folder as this homepage.
